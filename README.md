# Arthapalana portfolio intelligence

**Current investor policy:** A complete, source-backed company investment review is required before proposing a new buy, addition or re-entry. Numerical screening creates a shortlist; technical analysis supplies conditional timing within the investment thesis and valuation. Four scheduled AI batches remain, with up to two companies per batch and a 20-minute model budget. Missing research stays on the watchlist. Earlier implementation notes about AI-independent approval are superseded by this requirement.

Private portfolio dashboard for NocoDB `PortfolioTransactions`, with company research generated by the installed Antigravity (`agy`) CLI using its existing account login in Docker.

## Research and billing

The research runner uses AGY account authentication, excludes API keys from its child environment and sets `useG1Credits: false`. Direct Gemini/OpenAI API providers and paid-credit fallback are disabled. An expired login or exhausted account allowance stops research and preserves the last successful result. Antigravity account usage limits still apply.

Each turn runs in an isolated temporary profile with only public search/read and the scoped annual-report reader. Commands, filesystem access, browser actuation and delegation are excluded. The adapter requires schema-constrained output and observes tool progress without recording model reasoning. Account token refresh is retained in the private Docker mount; temporary conversations are removed.

## Data and recommendations

- Every NocoDB page is read; no records are changed and no trades are placed.
- FIFO lots, explicit CASH ledger, separate portfolios, household exposure and current-FX valuations.
- Yahoo market-session quotes, currency conversion, timestamps, price histories and corporate-action reconciliation checks. Prices can be delayed; the weekday-session check does not include an exchange holiday calendar.
- Declarative risk signals remain independent of the model. The runner applies the original Jarvis prompt with strict evidence and locked-position instructions, returning structured per-position recommendations.
- The server validates output, rejects unknown/duplicate positions, updates only scoped positions and retains other valid recommendations, preserves the ORCL ESPP lock and blocks unsupported buys/exits. Deterministic quantities are the execution reference.
- Forward XIRR, moat, governance, bank-specific metrics, news and full five-year forensic checks are unverified where not supplied. New-stock discovery runs the 18 Jarvis sector-aware sub-screens across NSE and NYSE/Nasdaq (A: quality compounders, B: GARP, C: value/re-rating). It retrieves paginated matches (250 per page) across Nasdaq Global Select/Global/Capital, NYSE, NYSE American and NSE, excluding all current household holdings and ORCL. It evaluates up to 2,000 unique companies per six-hour scan in resumable 32-company batches, with explicit pending and incomplete-coverage counts. Annual financial statements, forward EPS and analyst ratings feed the financial gate; 72-hour news and nearby earnings/dividend events are checked separately for affordable proposals. Missing evidence remains visible; passing a screen alone is not an entry recommendation.

## Architecture

Vinext / React / TypeScript on Cloudflare Workers, D1 persistence, owner-private Sites gateway. `/api/portfolio` serves live data; `/api/settings` saves model/timing; `/api/analysis` queues research and reads the last successful result. `/api/runner` is an outbound-only machine protocol protected by both the private Sites gateway and a separate shared secret.

The AI runner polls every 30 seconds. In Docker, the independent market service runs `runner/discovery.py` using yfinance, persists its SQLite progress in the `screening_data` volume and publishes financial evidence through a signed lease. The researcher receives the full numerical evidence for its focused company worklist; this bounded worklist never limits independent financial screening. The server downgrades watchlist/rejected names from entry. In the local Docker deployment, numerical eligibility and current event checks must be combined with a current, source-backed company investment review before buying; new entries use a 3–5% position range and existing additions target 10%. Sizing also requires fresh quotes/FX, whole Indian/four-decimal US shares, household caps, 25% cyclical / 20% commodity / 15% Category C limits, and a price-gap check. Unallocated or sub-meaningful amounts stay in reserve. This is conditional research, not automatic trade execution. A singleton D1 job queue, atomic claim and expiring/renewed lease prevent duplicate work. A 270-second screening timeout and twenty-minute model timeout prevents hung runs; upload retries reuse the same report and lease. Failed research never overwrites the last good report. Browser refreshes never make model calls directly.

Portfolio prices refresh every five minutes while the dashboard is open. Docker services keep working with the page closed while this machine is awake and connected. Automatic AI commentary runs at four weekday IST slots; technical/event checks run independently every 15 minutes during Indian or US market hours. Settings supports pausing automatic AI and choosing the subscription model. Manual AI research and market refreshes remain available at any time.

Automatic cash research persists a portfolio/market worklist in `research_campaigns` (migration 0012). Each of the four weekday slots—09:15, 11:15, 13:15 and 15:15 IST—claims up to two pending companies, for up to eight assessments per day. Untouched candidates advance before retries; failures or incomplete evidence wait at least 24 hours and are retried at most three times per evidence version. New reports or changed material evidence reopen the case without erasing its work history. Completed, current investment research is shared across portfolios. Enabled campaigns take turns, and the existing singleton lease prevents concurrent AI reviews. Outside these slots, research runs on demand only.

The top-level **Annual reports** page owns the shared company evidence library. It loads independently of portfolio data, links from company reviews and holdings, and reuses validated page-cited summaries rather than inserting full PDFs into every research request.

## Technical price plans

`/api/execution` accepts only current holdings or persisted screened candidates. It fetches one year of daily candles and five days of 5-minute candles, cached for 15 minutes; quote and session metadata refresh separately every minute. The numerical engine computes Wilder RSI/ATR(14), SMA(20/50/200), regular-session volume-weighted typical price, prior-session pivot levels and 20-session highs/lows. In-progress daily and 5-minute candles are excluded. Provider regular-session start/end timestamps determine open/closed status, including timezone/DST; execution drafts require current quotes and completed bars no more than 20 minutes old, at least 50 daily sessions and three volume-bearing intraday bars. The feed can be delayed; this is not an order-book or broker connection.

Price plans are available in holding details, Codex recommendation cards and discovery cards. Technicals refine existing research/concentration rules; they do not convert watchlist stocks into approved buys. Conditional buy zones use nearby support plus an ATR buffer, an ATR-based invalidation and resistance targets; a copyable buy draft additionally requires a non-falling trend, RSI below 70, reward/risk >=1.5, a nearby entry, sufficient cash and exposure headroom, current-session data, passing financial rules and a current event check without unreviewed flags. A supported company investment review is required before buying. A concentration trim uses the latest quote as its limit reference, recalculating quantity toward the 15% cap. A full-exit scenario is explicitly user-selected for a held position and is never represented as an AI exit recommendation. ORCL remains locked, and reconciliation problems block drafting. Large gaps beyond 3 ATRs block drafts for review.

Copying a draft writes only to the clipboard. Nothing is submitted to a broker; broker quotes, tick sizes, spreads and fractional-share support must be checked. Drafts expire within one minute (or sooner if the underlying quote ages out). Closed sessions produce next-session previews. This deterministic layer makes no Codex or OpenAI API calls. Tests in `tests/technicals.test.ts` and `tests/execution-api.test.mjs` cover indicator reference values, completed-bar isolation, session/freshness gates, current-price sizing, locked holdings and user-chosen exits.

### Broader discovery coverage

Yahoo/yfinance is the US numerical screening source; see [screen API](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.screen.html) and [exchange/query definitions](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.EquityQuery.html). The original Jarvis category/sector thresholds remain intact. Proxy or unit-ambiguous Yahoo ROCE/debt/interest filters are deferred to annual statement checks, rather than silently substituting a different ratio. Annual periods must align across income, cash-flow and balance statements, with 300–430-day comparatives and no future-dated or >550-day-old statements. ROCE uses EBIT / average (assets − current liabilities); interest coverage uses EBIT / absolute interest expense; long-term and total debt/equity are checked separately. Verified zero interest plus positive EBIT satisfies debt service without a fabricated infinite ratio. Each calculated metric retains its period, method and source inputs.

Safety limits: 250 results/page, 20 pages/query, 2,000 unique companies/scan and 32 company checks/batch (four parallel requests). Provider failures, repeated or overlapping pages, missing totals and capacity deferrals are shown as incomplete coverage. Company data failures are unverified, not financial rejections. A preliminary match is not proof of eligibility, and a zero-purchase plan never claims that no stock across Nasdaq qualifies. Financial screening can complete while qualitative/event evidence is still missing. Pending/missing/error counts appear beside the eligible/watchlist/rejected groups, with additional cards and exclusion explanations available through Show more.

Candidate snapshots are published atomically using D1 rows instead of a single large JSON field; the prior snapshot remains readable if a batch fails. Event/technical work shares capacity between portfolios and prioritizes old/unknown coverage so earlier alphabetical candidates cannot monopolize research. Alternatives are checked independently for affordability; only the actual allocation engine consumes shared cash and exposure headroom. Final trade readiness still requires fresh prices, current event review and a valid technical plan. No broker order is placed and no AI API billing is used for these checks.

Offline verification: `python3 scripts/test-logic.py` covers >250-result pagination, 320-company uploads, aligned annual calculations, interrupted/resumed batches, changed-criteria cache invalidation, atomic publication failure, per-company freshness, separate debt ratios, fair worklists and unchanged technical/AI schedules. ### News source recovery and event safeguards

The market service reads stock-specific **Yahoo Finance RSS**, then falls back to exact-company searches in **Google News RSS** and **Bing News RSS** when a source fails or returns unusable company coverage. It avoids the failing yfinance news POST endpoint. These feeds need no API keys and use no AI credits. Names come from verified ticker metadata; short ambiguous symbols such as AR and APP are not treated as ordinary-word company matches. Headlines are matched to the company, dated within 72 hours, deduplicated and checked for material events. All returned feed items are inspected before selecting display headlines; material headlines take priority. Rating changes, earnings, acquisitions, guidance, regulatory events and the existing risk terms still pause buying for review.

News and earnings/dividend calendar results are independently timestamped. The dashboard retains the selected news source, fallback attempts, failure reasons and calendar status. A fallback can restore news coverage, but never replace a failed calendar check, clear an existing event acknowledgement requirement, relax cash/technical rules or place an order. Both components must be current within 30 minutes. New or changed event evidence invalidates old acknowledgements. An all-source outage, malformed response, stale/unrelated-only feed, invalid calendar or evidence overflow leaves coverage unknown and buying paused. A valid, explicitly empty company search is distinguishable from an outage only when its feed-build timestamp is within 30 minutes; it is shown as no recent company headlines returned, not proof of comprehensive clean news.

Networking is bounded to eight seconds per request and a 210-second batch budget; unprocessed or unavailable stocks remain unknown. Publisher content is never fetched or executed by the reader. Existing 15-minute market checks, four daily AI slots and persistent Discord deduplication are unchanged. Regression checks cover real fallback-shaped payloads, all-source failure, malformed/stale/future/wrong-company stories, ambiguous tickers, material news after the first 15 items, calendar independence, review acknowledgements and source provenance persistence. Live probes are read-only; synthetic alerts are not sent.

News collection has its own bounded worklist of held action candidates and financially eligible discoveries. It continues independently of exchange-rate availability, cash, portfolio sizing or an open market session. Technical plans and purchase authorization retain their original cash, FX, exposure and session checks.


### Web availability and request isolation

Server caches retain completed data only, with bounded capacity and existing price/statement expiry times. Pending fetches and database queries may be deduplicated only within the same request through AsyncLocalStorage. A cancelled request cannot leave a globally shared I/O promise blocking later requests; late older loads cannot overwrite newer data, and invalidation prevents old in-flight loads from repopulating a cache.

`/api/health` performs a small read-only database probe. The Docker web supervisor allows 90 seconds for startup, probes every 20 seconds with a five-second timeout and terminates the web runtime after three consecutive failures. Docker's existing restart policy then recovers the web container. Healthy probes reset the failure count; normal operator stops still stop it. No Docker socket or application credentials are exposed to the health probe. Portfolio data and research/alert state remain on the existing volume.

### Investor policy: business quality before market timing

Migration 0010 adds persistent company investment reviews and a focused research ticker. A numerical screen is a shortlist, not a complete fundamental analysis. The local deployment requires a current supported company review for every new purchase, addition and re-entry. Profit-taking reviews also require a reviewed business outlook; concentration-risk trims and explicitly selected manual exits retain their separate safeguards. Technical momentum cannot establish a good company, clear missing fundamental evidence or justify an automatic full exit.

A review targets five consecutive annual periods of revenue, profit, operating cash flow, debt and equity; at least two aligned, source-backed periods and current primary results are mandatory. ### Uploaded annual reports and reusable evidence

Open **Annual reports** in the main navigation. The shared library supports every portfolio, holding review, recommendation and cash plan; company pages link directly to that stock's reports. Select the stock symbol, financial year ended and a searchable PDF; publication date is optional. Uploading saves and extracts the PDF locally without requesting AI. Use **Review company** to queue a focused review, or let its scheduled research batch process it. The company review is shared across portfolios, while quantities and purchase eligibility remain portfolio-specific.

The internal `documents` service uses Poppler to extract searchable pages once. Original bytes, page text and validated summaries persist in the `research_documents` Docker volume; the service exposes no host port and receives only a separate document-service secret. Files are limited to 50 MB, 1,000 pages, 1,000 reports and 10 GiB of original uploaded PDF bytes (extracted text and summaries use additional space). Password-protected/damaged PDFs fail explicitly; image-only scans need a searchable/OCR copy. OCR is not performed automatically.

SHA-256 identifies immutable files. Re-uploading identical bytes for the same stock/year reuses extraction and the cached summary. New bytes create a new version; the latest active version per stock/year is used. Removing a report archives it from active research; the original file remains in the private volume, and restoring identical bytes for the same stock/year reuses its summary. A changed or removed source invalidates an investment case that depended on it. A library outage pauses purchase authorization rather than hiding changes to source evidence.

The first review searches and reads specific PDF pages, then produces a bounded summary of business quality, accounting findings, risks, gaps and annual financial facts. A local validator checks report hash, issuer/year quotations and physical PDF page references. Financial units are recorded explicitly and amounts normalized to base currency units. This verifies the cited text exists; it does not independently audit every conclusion or authenticate the uploaded document. Review figures citing uploads must reconcile to the cached facts. Missing older history is a warning when the mandatory aligned periods are available; unresolved material discrepancies and current-event concerns remain purchase blockers.

Later reviews receive a compact evidence pack, capped at roughly 30,000 characters for the two-company batch, containing the latest findings, older exceptions and up to six annual periods per company. Full PDF text is never placed in the routine prompt. Unchanged summaries cannot be regenerated in the output schema. A scoped MCP reader can retrieve a cached summary, search snippets or 1–4 specific pages when an unresolved question needs detail; cached reports have an eight-page budget, repeated reads are suppressed, and text/search budgets apply across concurrent requests. Initial uncached reports have a larger bounded reading budget. Reviews reuse validated summaries until the file/version changes; current prices, ratios, subsequent results and events still come from fresh market/public sources. Routine 15-minute technical checks do not run AI or read report text.

### Materiality and report-summary repair

The `materiality-v2` policy separates mandatory blockers from secondary warnings. Any serious accounting red flag, unverified accounting, unmanaged material concern, unsupported solvency or missing usable valuation blocks a purchase. Source-backed, mitigated minor concerns can remain warnings. Price, events, cash, Jarvis financial thresholds, exposure and anti-churn safeguards continue to run independently. Share-lot rounding applies the minimum practical allocation without exceeding cash or position caps. This does not automatically approve a company or place an order.

Annual reports are prepared before the investment call, with one focused repair attempt using structured field/page diagnostics and the previous extraction draft. Each attempt uses a fresh ephemeral Codex context; the repair receives the saved draft instead of trying to resume a non-persistent session. Legitimate short printed units (for example `INR Mn`) and up to eight literal financial quotations are supported. An invalid summary remains unavailable and stops the investment call; it is never silently promoted to verified evidence. Validated summaries are reused in a fresh investment context and the cached-report eight-page budget is restored after preparation. Full PDF text is not inserted in routine review prompts.

Cards show a short decision, up to three primary reasons and concise warnings; full findings remain expandable. A completed AI run reports how many investment cases are ready and how many still need evidence. Completion alone never means the stock is approved.
